Join the club

Membership that fits your game.

Membership fees cover 12 months from July. Reduced pro-rata fees apply when joining later in the year.

Junior (to 18)

$155 per year

  • Must be accompanied by an adult at the club.
  • Eligible for all club competitions; full use of facilities.
  • No access card is issued to junior members; a parent may be responsible for the card.

Student (to 25)

$240 per year

  • For one full-time student aged 25 or under.
  • In-house and/or Pennant competitions; full use of facilities.

Associate

$370 per year

  • For one adult playing socially and in the annual Club Championships.
  • Full use of club facilities.

Full Member

$500 per year

  • For one adult playing in-house and/or Pennant competitions.
  • Full use of club rooms and facilities.

Associate Family

$480 per year

  • Access when courts are available.
  • Competition eligibility is limited to Club Championships.

Family

$570 per year

  • One adult member and any junior can play in-house or intra-club.
  • Other family members may play when courts are available, as per Associate membership.

Family +

$710 per year

  • For two adults and their school-age dependent children.
  • Any family member may compete in in-house and Pennant competitions; full use of facilities.

Pennant Only

$240 per year

  • Access only for inter-club Pennant competitions, including Club Circuit, Masters and ESRA.
  • No regular club access.

Annual on-time payment discounts are offered; ask the club for current details.

Good to know

Do I need to have played squash or racquetball before?

Not at all. Contact the club to ask about beginner-friendly ways to get involved.

Can I come and look around?

Yes, contact the club to arrange a suitable time to visit.

Can children join?

Yes, the club has a junior program. Ask about age requirements, availability and supervision.

How do I become a member?

Get in touch and we'll guide you through the available membership options and application process.
